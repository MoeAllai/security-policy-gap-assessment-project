# Policy Inventory

## Purpose

This policy inventory documents the security policies reviewed for CloudCore Analytics as part of the security policy gap assessment.

The goal is to identify policy ownership, review cadence, approval status, and overall maturity.

## Inventory Observations

The policy inventory shows that CloudCore Analytics has a basic security policy set, but several policies need improvement.

Common issues include:

- Missing or unclear policy owners
- Missing review dates
- Draft policies not formally approved
- Weak evidence retention requirements
- Incomplete exception handling
- Limited framework mapping
- Control requirements not written clearly enough for audit readiness

## Analyst Summary

CloudCore Analytics has several important policies in place, which is a positive foundation. However, the policy set is not fully mature because some policies are outdated, incomplete, or not specific enough to support audit readiness.

The recommended next step is to perform a detailed gap assessment and create a corrective action plan for high-priority policy weaknesses.
