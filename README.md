# Security Policy Gap Assessment Project

## Project Overview

This project simulates a Junior GRC Analyst assignment focused on reviewing security policies for a mock SaaS company and identifying gaps against common cybersecurity frameworks.

The goal is to demonstrate practical GRC skills in policy review, gap assessment, control mapping, risk identification, corrective action planning, and executive reporting.

## Mock Scenario

CloudCore Analytics is a 120-employee SaaS company preparing to improve its information security governance program.

The company has several security policies, but leadership is concerned that the policies may be incomplete, outdated, or not fully aligned with recognized frameworks such as NIST Cybersecurity Framework and ISO/IEC 27001.

A Junior GRC Analyst is assigned to review the current policy set, identify gaps, map gaps to framework concepts, and recommend corrective actions.

## Project Scope

The review covers the following policy areas:

- Information security policy
- Access control policy
- Password and MFA policy
- Incident response policy
- Vendor risk management policy
- Data classification policy
- Acceptable use policy
- Business continuity policy
- Change management policy
- Evidence retention and audit documentation policy

## Key Findings Summary

The review identified several governance and policy gaps, including:

- Policies do not have documented owners
- Several policies do not show annual review dates
- Access control policy does not clearly require quarterly access reviews
- MFA requirements are not fully documented
- Incident response policy does not define customer notification expectations
- Vendor risk policy does not define high-risk vendor review requirements
- Data classification policy does not clearly define restricted data handling
- Business continuity policy does not require annual testing
- Evidence retention expectations are not standardized

## Analyst Conclusion

CloudCore Analytics has a basic security policy set, but the policies need stronger ownership, review cadence, control requirements, and framework alignment.

The highest-priority remediation items are access control requirements, MFA documentation, incident response notification requirements, vendor risk review requirements, data classification handling, and evidence retention standards.

This project demonstrates how a Junior GRC Analyst can support security governance by reviewing policies, identifying gaps, mapping requirements to frameworks, and building a corrective action plan.
