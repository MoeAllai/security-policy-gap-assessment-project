# ISO 27001 Mapping

## Purpose

This document maps identified security policy gaps to ISO/IEC 27001-style information security control concepts.

The goal is to show how policy weaknesses relate to governance, access control, incident response, supplier security, data protection, business continuity, and audit readiness.

---

## ISO 27001 Mapping Table

| Gap ID | Policy Area | Gap Summary | ISO 27001-Style Concept | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GAP-01 | Policy Governance | Some policies do not clearly identify a policy owner. | Information security roles and responsibilities | Policies need owners to maintain, approve, enforce, and update them. |
| GAP-02 | Policy Governance | Several policies do not include documented annual review dates. | Information security policy review | Policies should be reviewed regularly to remain current and effective. |
| GAP-03 | Access Control | Quarterly access reviews are not clearly required. | User access rights review | Regular access reviews help ensure users only keep appropriate access. |
| GAP-04 | Privileged Access | Privileged access requirements are not clearly defined. | Privileged access rights management | Admin access should be approved, justified, limited, and reviewed. |
| GAP-05 | Password and MFA | MFA exception handling is unclear. | Authentication information and secure access | Authentication exceptions should be approved, tracked, time-bound, and monitored. |
| GAP-06 | Incident Response | Customer notification expectations are not clearly defined. | Information security incident management | Incident communication requirements help ensure timely escalation and response. |
| GAP-07 | Vendor Risk Management | High-risk vendor review requirements are incomplete. | Supplier relationship security | Vendors handling sensitive data should be reviewed based on risk level. |
| GAP-08 | Data Classification | Restricted data handling requirements are not detailed enough. | Information classification and handling | Sensitive data requires clear rules for storage, access, transfer, retention, and deletion. |
| GAP-09 | Business Continuity | Annual BCP/DR testing is not clearly required. | ICT readiness for business continuity | Recovery plans should be tested to confirm operational resilience. |
| GAP-10 | Change Management | Emergency change approval and post-review requirements are incomplete. | Change management | Emergency changes should be documented, approved, and reviewed after implementation. |
| GAP-11 | Evidence Retention | Evidence naming and storage standards are not standardized. | Documented information and audit evidence | Compliance evidence should be organized, retained, and easy to validate. |
| GAP-12 | Acceptable Use | Enforcement responsibilities are not clearly assigned. | Acceptable use and disciplinary process | Policy enforcement responsibilities help ensure consistent handling of violations. |

---

## ISO-Aligned Improvement Summary

| Area | Recommended Improvement |
|---|---|
| Governance | Assign policy owners and require annual review/approval history |
| Access Control | Define quarterly access review and privileged access requirements |
| Authentication | Create formal MFA exception management process |
| Incident Response | Define customer notification criteria and escalation timeline |
| Supplier Security | Define high-risk vendor review requirements and evidence expectations |
| Data Protection | Strengthen restricted data handling requirements |
| Business Continuity | Require annual BCP/DR testing and evidence retention |
| Change Management | Add emergency change documentation and post-review requirements |
| Evidence Management | Standardize audit evidence naming, storage, ownership, and retention |
| Acceptable Use | Assign enforcement responsibilities to HR, IT, and management |

---

## High-Priority ISO-Aligned Gaps

The highest priority policy gaps are:

1. Access reviews are not clearly required
2. Privileged access requirements are incomplete
3. MFA exception handling is unclear
4. Incident notification expectations are undefined
5. High-risk vendor review requirements are incomplete
6. Restricted data handling requirements are weak
7. Evidence retention standards are not standardized

## Analyst Summary

The ISO-style mapping shows that CloudCore Analytics has several policy gaps connected to governance, access control, supplier security, incident response, data protection, business continuity, and evidence management.

The most urgent improvements should focus on making policy requirements clear, measurable, enforceable, and auditable.

This helps the company prepare for audits, customer security reviews, and internal governance maturity improvements.
