# Policy References

## Purpose

This document lists frameworks, concepts, and simulated evidence connected to the Security Policy Gap Assessment Project.

The project is a mock GRC portfolio project and does not use confidential company data.

---

## Frameworks and Standards Referenced

### NIST Cybersecurity Framework

The NIST Cybersecurity Framework helps organizations manage cybersecurity risk through the functions of Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover.

This project connects to NIST CSF concepts such as:

- Governance and policy oversight
- Roles and responsibilities
- Identity and access management
- Data security
- Supply chain risk management
- Incident response communication
- Recovery planning
- Audit readiness and oversight

---

### ISO/IEC 27001 Concepts

ISO/IEC 27001 focuses on building and maintaining an information security management system.

This project connects to ISO 27001-style concepts such as:

- Information security policies
- Roles and responsibilities
- Access control
- Privileged access management
- Authentication requirements
- Supplier relationship security
- Information classification
- Incident management
- Business continuity
- Change management
- Documented information and evidence retention

---

### Security Governance Concepts

This project demonstrates practical security governance concepts, including:

- Policy ownership

- Approval history
- Version control
- Policy enforcement responsibilities
- Exception management

- Corrective action planning
- Evidence retention

## Policies Reviewed

| Policy | Review Focus |

| Information Security Policy | Governance responsibilities, ownership, and oversight |
| Access Control Policy | Access reviews, privileged access, approval, and access removal |

| Data Classification Policy | Restricted data handling rules |
| Acceptable Use Policy | Employee responsibilities and enforcement |
| Business Continuity Policy | BCP/DR testing and evidence requirements |
