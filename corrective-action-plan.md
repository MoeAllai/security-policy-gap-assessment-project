# Corrective Action Plan

## Purpose

This corrective action plan documents recommended remediation activities for security policy gaps identified during the policy gap assessment for CloudCore Analytics.

The goal is to assign ownership, prioritize policy updates, define timelines, and identify evidence needed to close each gap.

---

## Corrective Action Plan Table

| Action ID | Related Gap | Corrective Action | Priority | Owner | Target Timeline | Status | Evidence Needed for Closure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CAP-01 | GAP-01 | Assign a documented owner for every security policy. Owners may be named individuals or role-based owners such as IT Manager, Compliance Manager, or IT Security Lead. | High | Compliance Manager | 15 days | Open | Updated policy inventory showing owner for each policy |
| CAP-02 | GAP-02 | Add last review date, next review date, version history, and approval history to all security policies. | High | Compliance Manager | 30 days | Open | Updated policies with review dates and approval history |
| CAP-03 | GAP-03 | Update the Access Control Policy to require quarterly access reviews for critical systems and systems containing sensitive data. | High | IT Manager | 30 days | Open | Updated Access Control Policy with quarterly review requirement |
| CAP-04 | GAP-04 | Add privileged access requirements, including approval, business justification, least privilege, quarterly review, and documented removal when no longer needed. | High | IT Security | 30 days | Open | Updated Access Control Policy section for privileged access |
| CAP-05 | GAP-05 | Create a formal MFA exception process requiring owner, approval, justification, compensating control, review date, and expiration date. | High | IT Security | 30 days | Open | Updated Password and MFA Policy plus MFA exception template |
| CAP-06 | GAP-06 | Update the Incident Response Policy to define customer notification criteria, escalation path, and target notification timeline. | High | IT Security | 45 days | Open | Updated Incident Response Policy with notification requirements |
| CAP-07 | GAP-07 | Update the Vendor Risk Management Policy to define vendor risk tiers, high-risk vendor evidence requirements, review frequency, and approval criteria. | High | Compliance | 45 days | Open | Updated Vendor Risk Management Policy and vendor review checklist |
| CAP-08 | GAP-08 | Strengthen the Data Classification Policy by defining restricted data handling requirements for storage, transmission, access, sharing, retention, and deletion. | High | Compliance + Privacy | 45 days | Open | Updated Data Classification Policy with restricted data handling rules |
| CAP-09 | GAP-09 | Update the Business Continuity Policy to require annual BCP/DR testing and retention of test evidence. | Medium | Operations | 60 days | Open | Updated Business Continuity Policy and BCP/DR test evidence template |
| CAP-10 | GAP-10 | Update the Change Management Policy to define emergency change approval, documentation, and post-implementation review requirements. | Medium | Engineering Manager | 60 days | Open | Updated Change Management Policy with emergency change process |
| CAP-11 | GAP-11 | Create a standard evidence retention process, including folder structure, naming convention, retention period, owner, and acceptable evidence types. | High | Compliance | 45 days | Open | Evidence Retention Policy, evidence folder template, naming standard |
| CAP-12 | GAP-12 | Update the Acceptable Use Policy to define enforcement responsibilities for HR, IT, managers, and employees. | Medium | HR + IT | 60 days | Open | Updated Acceptable Use Policy with enforcement responsibilities |

---

## Priority Definitions

| Priority | Meaning |
|---|---|
| High | Should be addressed first because the gap may affect audit readiness, security risk, or compliance expectations |
| Medium | Should be addressed after high-priority items but still requires formal remediation |
| Low | Lower urgency but should be tracked for policy maturity improvement |

---

## Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Open | Corrective action has been identified but work has not started |
| In Progress | Owner is actively working on the corrective action |
| Pending Review | Updated policy or evidence is ready for review |
| Closed | Evidence has been reviewed and corrective action is complete |
| Risk Accepted | Management has accepted the remaining risk |

---

## Recommended Remediation Sequence

The recommended remediation order is:

1. Assign policy owners and review dates
2. Update Access Control Policy requirements
3. Define privileged access requirements
4. Formalize MFA exception management
5. Update Incident Response notification requirements
6. Complete Vendor Risk Management requirements
7. Strengthen Data Classification handling rules
8. Standardize Evidence Retention requirements
9. Update Business Continuity testing requirements
10. Improve Change Management and Acceptable Use language

---

## Analyst Notes

The most urgent corrective actions are related to access control, privileged access, MFA, incident response, vendor risk, data classification, and evidence retention.

These areas are prioritized because they are commonly reviewed during audits, customer security reviews, and internal risk assessments.

The corrective action plan should be reviewed with policy owners and leadership to confirm timelines, responsibilities, and closure evidence.
